Themes × deities, round 3

Five themes, all with light and dark. Kalamkārī's night version uses muted, earthy dyes on iron-black cloth. In dark mode, every deity accent is muted and pulled slightly toward olive. Each theme now has a signature applied to every element, not just a border. The deity still sets the accent hue for the title and top band; in Kalamkārī the other roles use fixed dye colours.

Dark-mode glows: Paurṇamī has cool moonlight from above. Kalamkārī has the four dyes bleeding softly in from the corners. Tāḍapatra has a low oil-lamp glow beneath the leaf. Kuṅkuma has ember warmth from the top-left. Vṛndā has green moonlight through a canopy.
Signatures: Kalamkārī uses multicolour dye roles (indigo headings, madder words, leaf-green grammar, myrobalan labels) with a printed band border and a four-dye rule. Paurṇamī has a moon-glow behind the title, a soft halo on cards and a silver rule. Tāḍapatra has ruled leaf lines, a double margin, leaf-ended cards and ink headings. Kuṅkuma has a deeper base and tilaka bars on headings and cards. Vṛndā has a deeper green, leaf-cut corners and a vine rule.
